# GOCs HR — Employee Mobile App User Manual

**Product:** GOCs HR Mobile App (Android)  
**Audience:** Employees & Team Leads  
**Version:** 1.0 (Demo / Handover)  
**Built by:** Digital Dive Technologies  

---

## 1. Introduction

The **GOCs HR mobile app** is for **employees only**. HR administrators use the **web portal** instead.

With the app you can:

- Punch attendance (biometric where supported)
- Apply for leave and track status
- View payslips
- Request certificates (Salary, Bank, NOC Travel)
- Read HR notifications
- Browse company directory and your documents

**Team leads** additionally approve their team’s leave requests.

---

## 2. Installation

1. Install the APK provided by HR/IT: `app-release.apk`
2. On Android, allow **Install from unknown sources** if prompted.
3. Open **GOCs HR**.

> iPhone/iOS: not in current demo scope — Android APK only.

## 4. ESS / Home dashboard

After login you land on **ESS / Home**.

| Section | Purpose |
|---------|---------|
| **Welcome** | Your name and role |
| **Biometric punch** | Today’s check-in/out (fingerprint/face if device supports) |
| **Leave balances** | Remaining annual/sick days |
| **Latest payslip** | Quick view of last salary — tap for full list |
| **Quick actions** | Shortcuts to Leave, Payslips, Certificates, Directory, etc. |

Pull down to **refresh** data.

---

## 5. Apply for leave

**Menu → My Leaves**

1. Review your **leave balances** at the top.
2. Select **leave type** (e.g. Annual, Sick).
3. Enter **start date**, **end date**, and **number of days**.
4. Add **reason** (optional but recommended).
5. Tap **Submit request**.

### 5.1 What happens next

| Stage | Who | Where |
|-------|-----|-------|
| 1 | Your request is sent | Status: Pending manager |
| 2 | Team lead approves/rejects | **Team approvals** on their app |
| 3 | HR final approval | HR **portal** (not app) |
| Done | You get notification | **Alerts** menu |

You can track status in **My Leaves** list. Do not tap Submit twice — wait for confirmation.

---

## 6. Team approvals (Team leads only)

If you manage direct reports, you will see **Team approvals** in the menu.

**Menu → Team approvals**

1. Review pending leave cards (employee name, type, dates).
2. Tap **Approve** or **Reject**.
3. Optional: add a note.

After you approve, the request goes to **HR for final approval**. The employee receives a notification.

Demo: Fatima reports to Ahmed — use Ahmed’s login to test.

---

## 7. Attendance

**Menu → My Attendance**

- View your punch history.
- From **ESS Home**, use **Check in** / **Check out** with biometric verification when available.

If biometric fails, contact HR — do not share your login with others.

---

## 8. Payslips

**Menu → My Payslips** (or ESS → View slips)

- Lists salary slips by period.
- Shows basic, overtime, allowances, deductions, **net pay**.
- WPS reference shown for UAE payroll employees.

Payslips appear after HR runs monthly payroll on the portal.

---

## 9. Certificate requests

**Menu → Certificates** (or ESS → Request certificate)

### 9.1 Request steps

1. Review **auto-filled profile** (name, ID, designation, salary, division).
2. Choose **certificate type**:
   - **Salary Certificate**
   - **Bank Certificate** — enter **bank name**
   - **NOC (Travel)** — enter **travel destination**
3. Add **purpose / comments** (optional).
4. Tap **Submit request**.

### 9.2 After submission

- Status shows in **My requests** (Pending → Issued / Rejected).
- HR processes requests on the **portal**.
- When **Issued**, you receive an **Alerts** notification.
- Collect official copy from HR if required.

You cannot approve your own certificate — HR only.

---

## 10. Notifications (Alerts)

**Menu → Notifications**

Shows HR messages: leave submitted/approved/rejected, certificate updates, reminders.

Open Alerts after submitting leave or certificates to confirm the system received your request.

## 12. Logout & security

- **Menu → Profile → Logout** when using a shared device.
- Do not share your password.
- Report lost phones to HR immediately.

---

## 13. What employees cannot do in the app

These are **portal-only** (HR admin):

- Add or bulk-import employees  
- Create divisions  
- Run payroll or download WPS/bank files  
- Final HR leave approval  
- Issue certificate PDFs

## 14. Troubleshooting

| Issue | Action |
|-------|--------|
| “No internet” message | Connect to Wi‑Fi or mobile data |
| Session expired | Log in again |
| Leave submit error | Check dates; avoid duplicate pending request |
| No team approvals menu | You are not configured as a team lead — contact HR |
| Old version / missing Certificates | Install latest APK from HR |
